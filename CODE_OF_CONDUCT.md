# Code of Conduct

## Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and inclusive community, we as
contributors and maintainers pledge to making participation in our project and
our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, national
origin, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our
community:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (doxing) without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, trolling, or harassing behavior may be handled by
removing comments, warnings, or temporary or permanent bans as determined by
the maintainers of this project.

Maintenance staff have the right to remove, edit, or delete comments,
commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not
aligned with this Code of Conduct, or to ban temporarily or permanently any
contributor for other behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or
offensive.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all content within this project's repository,
GitHub wiki, and any other official project spaces. It also applies when an
individual is representing the project or its community in public.

## Enforcement Contact

- Primary: GitHub profile of the maintainer
- Secondary: Open a repository issue requesting a private channel

Do not invent an email address for enforcement contact.
